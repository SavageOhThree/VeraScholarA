SECTION 01

Withdrawal & attendance

Sample agreement wording · Source text

Nonattendance does not constitute withdrawal. A student must submit the designated withdrawal form. Charges remain payable unless a refund is available under the applicable refund schedule.

Stopping attendance does not automatically withdraw you. You must submit the withdrawal form. You still owe the charges unless the refund schedule allows a refund.

Need more clarity? Registrar. Ask for the withdrawal form and refund schedule. Those details are not included in this sample.
SECTION 02

Third-party access

Sample agreement wording · Source text

The student portal provider may process student-submitted files solely to deliver the portal service and may not use those files for advertising.

The company running the portal may handle files you submit only to provide the portal service. This clause does not allow it to use those files for advertising.

Need more clarity? College privacy office. Ask about retention, deletion, or other uses. This sample does not answer those questions.
SECTION 03

Estimated financial assistance

Sample agreement wording · Source text

An estimate of financial assistance is not a guarantee of disbursement. Amounts and timing remain subject to eligibility verification.

An estimated aid amount does not promise that the money will be paid out. The amount and payment timing still depend on checking your eligibility.

Need more clarity? Financial aid office. Ask which eligibility checks remain and when to expect an update. No payment date is specified in this sample.
